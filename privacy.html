KMNG TV privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026 · KMNG TV home

KMNG TV is a personal app with one user: its owner. It runs on his own computer at home. This page says what it does with information from his Google account.

What it reads and changes

Only through YouTube's official interface, and only with the permission its owner gives when he signs in:

  • his channel's name and id, each time it runs, to make sure it is working on his own account;
  • the one playlist called "KMNG TV": reading it, adding and removing its videos, and keeping its description up to date (his other playlists are never changed);
  • his channel, to upload the station's own short DJ clips (unlisted);
  • whether each video in that playlist is still available, and its view count, to know how far the playlist has been watched.

It also looks up public video information (titles, channels and lengths of public music videos), which needs no sign-in.

How it is used

Only to keep the "KMNG TV" playlist. It does not read his watch history, subscriptions, comments, messages or any other part of his account, and it never posts, comments or likes.

Where it is kept

On the owner's own computer only. The sign-in permission is stored in a file only his user account can read. Information read from YouTube is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as YouTube's rules for developers require. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Sharing

Nothing is shared, sold or transferred to anyone. KMNG TV has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

KMNG TV's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Taking the permission back

The permission can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting KMNG TV's files on the owner's computer removes everything it stored.
