KMNG TV

A personal music-video channel, kept by KMNG, one family's home radio station.

KMNG TV keeps one YouTube playlist, called "KMNG TV", on its owner's own YouTube account. It holds the official music videos, and official live and acoustic performances, of the songs he loves, with short clips from the station's DJs between them. The family watches it on the TV in the YouTube app.

It runs on its owner's own computer at home. It is not a public service: it has one user, its owner, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does with YouTube

  • Looks up public music videos through YouTube's official interface, to find each song's official video and official live or acoustic versions.
  • With its owner's permission, keeps the "KMNG TV" playlist on his account up to date.
  • With its owner's permission, uploads the station's own short DJ clips to his channel, unlisted.
  • With its owner's permission, checks each video in that playlist is still available and reads its view count, so the playlist knows how far the family has watched.

It never reads anything else on the account, never posts or comments, and shows no ads.
